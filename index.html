How to play

You start with two cards. Draw another card to try to reach exactly 21. Go over 21 and the round ends. You can start a fresh round whenever you like.

This follows the simplified Scrimba lesson in which: Aces always count as 11, jacks, queens and kings all count as 10. Each draw is random, so cards can repeat. There's no dealer or betting.
